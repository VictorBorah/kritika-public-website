The 48V/60A E-Rickshaw Controller (CY) is designed to manage motor operation in compatible 48V E-Rickshaw and electric three-wheeler systems. It works between the battery, throttle, and motor, processing the driver's input and regulating electrical power delivered to the motor for controlled acceleration and speed.

With a 48 -60V system and 60A current rating, this controller is suitable for compatible E-Rickshaw applications requiring dependable motor control and responsive throttle operation. It can be used for EV assembly, replacement, servicing, and maintenance.

Compatibility Note
Before installation, verify the motor power, operating voltage, current rating, Hall/phase configuration, connector type, wiring, throttle compatibility, and protection functions against the manufacturer's specifications. Proper matching of the controller with the complete EV system is important for safe and reliable operation.
